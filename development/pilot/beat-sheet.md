## Episode question and shape

**Question:** Can Callie find a version of her father she can bury without letting him choose the story again?

**Answer:** She chooses a case instead of a goodbye—and recruits living people into a damaging assumption before she has heard them.

Approximately **58 minutes**, 40 scenes, six sequences. Timings are editorial targets, not measured runtime. Present action occupies about 39 minutes; the historical line about 19. The past is an independent marriage/work story whose choices make the present's language of inheritance inadequate; it does not explain the wreck.

## Load-bearing turns

1. **Opening hook:** A widow removes a ludicrous humanitarian award from car-wreck possessions and hides it. No second crime is implied; this is a first lie about the man everyone must remember.
2. **Inciting disturbance:** Leon's private meeting gives Callie a problem she can work instead of a loss she cannot. She insists on it and owns the damage.
3. **Midpoint:** Caldwell names and Ruth's visible knowledge let Callie manufacture a simple scandal. Ben/Leah hear it; Ruth must endure it; Leon can no longer assume reading is neutral.
4. **Historical reversal:** Marian removes her ledger and vetoes wide disclosure. Caleb loses public force; she risks his anger and delays a remedy the household needs. A later plan must account for her, not merely protect her.
5. **Present cost:** Callie's voicemail destroys an easy first contact. She also refuses Leah's memorial-photo request. Her skill already costs intimacy; the firm referral opportunity is seeded but not yet lost.
6. **Climactic choice:** Callie separates questions: independent counsel can assess a challenge; Leon can answer a voluntary request; Sarah can decide whether to meet. She is still angry and controlling. Her moral arc has begun, not completed.
7. **End hook:** Callie enters Episode 2 without the right to define the Caldwells. The audience has met Caleb/Marian and knows what her neat label cannot contain; it has not seen their fate.

## Reveal and withholding budget

**Reveal:** Ordinary death; contradictory Eli; award's face and apparent absurdity; Ruth's concealment; objects and conditional instructions; Caldwell names/Ruth's recognition; Caleb's family/work complaint; Marian's livelihood/refusal; Callie's career resentment through a firm interruption and old marked career material.

**Withhold:** Award's hidden inscription/provenance/purpose; video content; Caldwell arrival and full paternity correction; storage contents; payments; what was filed historically, if anything; leak/threats; killing; silence bargain; intact photograph; hidden Callie box; contingency paper; finale. A folded photograph is among the objects but is not identified as Caleb or investigated now.

No contest period is quoted, admission established, valid ground selected or contest filed. Eli's fictional condition is Leon's personal undertaking, not a disclosure order. Episode 2's possible filing remains contingent on a viable chosen story ground and admission path. Dinner is Eli's wish; nobody claims skipping it legally forfeits inheritance.
