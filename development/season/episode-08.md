# Episode 08 — The Photograph Uncut

Status: provisional architecture test card; corrective revision by Alex; not an episode draft or approved canon. Owner: Alex.

- Canon version: provisional architecture-test-v2; session-authorized working direction, not approved canon.
- Protagonist objective and stakes: Callie wants the original photograph and letters available to a record the Caldwells govern. Leon wants to correct Eli’s legend without exposing material he believes could harm survivors. Daniel initially wants the photograph kept outside the family’s story as well as outside public use. The stakes are family control and public naming, not asset accounting.
- Contemporary timeline conflict and choices: Callie finds the hidden box, unsent letters and an intact photograph of Eli, Leon and Caleb. Daniel withholds his own participation and name from public use and first argues that the photograph should remain unrepresented; he does not claim legal control over the photograph. Sarah wants a private copy. Leon admits the cut photograph circulated but not who cut it. Daniel reverses course by writing a first-person note for the private Caldwell copy: he names what he remembers of his father and Eli, including the unresolved complexity of their relationship, without claiming to explain the image or authorize its use. He asks that the note stay with the family copy even if it complicates the family’s preferred account. He gives up the protective blankness of leaving his father’s connection unrecorded and accepts the risk that his own memory will be judged alongside the photograph. **Invented story beat, not procedure:** this is a family act of remembering, not a legal or publication authorization; the separate post-admission contest creates no access, custody, or disclosure step. **Non-substitutable outcome:** Sarah may keep a private family copy on her own terms, while Daniel contributes his own account; neither act authorizes public naming or image use. **Changed option:** Episode 09 must contend with Daniel as an active author of the family record, not only a refusal; no agreement may treat his note as Sarah’s consent or as proof of the photograph’s provenance.
- Historical timeline conflict and choices: **Provisional aftermath sequence, not fact.** Marian chooses what name and image survive for the children and rejects Eli’s proposed caption or memorial plan. She conflicts with Eli/Leon by refusing their controlled archive, while preserving a family copy under her own conditions. Character belief: Eli thinks an image can repair the record; Marian thinks an image without family authority repeats possession. Surviving evidence: photograph, letters and a cut image may establish artifacts and competing custody. Unknown: provenance, caption, cutter and legal publication authority.
- Why these timelines meet in this episode: The intact photograph gives Caleb a face but not Callie ownership of it. Restoration itself may be paternalistic.
- Character turns and consequences: Daniel moves from protective silence to private authorship, accepting that his own complicated memory belongs beside—not underneath—the contested image. He risks family judgment and commits not to erase the account if it troubles a clean story. Sarah’s separate choice about a private copy persists; Leon distinguishes memory from documentary proof; Callie gives up the clean public image.
- Clues, audience belief and reliability: Box, letters, photograph and “He trusted me” are source-backed fiction; provenance and custody are unresolved. HIST-1965-009 supports treating the case as fictional/composite, not identifying a real event.
- Major beats:
  - Callie discovers the box labeled for her.
  - Leon arrives before unrestricted copying.
  - Daniel refuses public use, then writes a first-person note for the private family copy; Sarah requests her own private copy.
  - Marian rejects an Eli-authored caption or memorial plan.
  - Callie offers the original to the Caldwells.
- Ending and next-episode pressure: Sarah accepts a private copy without granting broad publication permission; Daniel’s note travels with the family copy as his own account, not a release. **Changed option:** Episode 09 must negotiate with two siblings who have acted in distinct ways—Sarah over her copy and Daniel over his memory—while public naming remains divided and cannot be solved by the estate.
