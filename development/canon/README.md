# Story canon and continuity

Maya owns these records. No story revision is approved merely because it appears here.

Use statuses: explicit-brief, source-backed, proposed, approved-canon, documented-fact, unverified or contested. Fiction canon and historical/legal evidence remain distinct. Assign stable IDs and identify source location or approval provenance.

[characters.csv](characters.csv) records ages/relationships; [chronology.csv](chronology.csv) records events in story order; [reveals.csv](reveals.csv) records discovery order and knowledge changes. The preserved `source-inventory-v1` rows remain unchanged. They include the original treatment's alternatives and causal claims, not all of which are retained in the current development version.

The current writing version is **MM-DP-1**. [Alex's approval record](../brief/creative-approvals.md) approves its setting, age/name matrix, depot complaint and single due-execution contest design as fictional writing canon. Rows marked `MM-DP-1-approved-frame` carry that narrower approval. Expanded `MM-DP-1` rows remain a substantial story submission until the applicable independent review and Alex decision are recorded. The row key is stable ID plus version; do not merge a source row and a development row into a new historical assertion.

[Package reconciliation](package-reconciliation.md) identifies source departures, age arithmetic, pilot handoff and the current decision limits. [Custody and consent](custody-consent.csv) tracks individual materials and permitted actions separately from ownership or legal-right findings. The [development bible](../../package/development-bible.md), [complete season treatment](../../package/season-treatment.md) and ten expanded episode cards use these records; Charlie's approved outline and reviewed Fountain master govern pilot execution.

The older [season-break reconciliation](season-break-reconciliation.md) remains a historical review record. Its pending/blocked language and calls for research or consultation as prerequisites are superseded by the current creative brief, governance and dated creative approvals; retaining that record does not reinstate those gates. The preserved source file is never edited to reconcile development changes.
