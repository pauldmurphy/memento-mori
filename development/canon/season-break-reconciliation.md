## Continuity status and gate

**Safe for further provisional design:** Yes, conditionally. The provisional break and cards can support additional architecture testing if every date, age, legal mechanism, object provenance, payment term, Marian detail and causal link remains labeled as source-backed fiction, proposed design, character belief, artifact, or unknown. The current separation of estate terms from disclosure consent and the insistence that Daniel and Sarah can refuse are continuity safeguards, not settled law or canon.

**Items that block a locked season break:**

1. A reconciled year/relative chronology and age ledger, especially Daniel/Sarah birth timing and current ages; no ages may be inferred from “newborn,” “1965,” or the sixty-year echo.
2. A decision about whether Marian Bell Caldwell’s name, biography and reading-fund history remain a proposal, with research/consultation sufficient to avoid silently replacing the unnamed source character.
3. Item-level provenance and custody for the award, pen, photograph(s), letters, case materials, video, ledgers, checks, trusts/charity records and firm sign.
4. A non-overstated payment and relationship chronology: channels, recipients, terms, Ruth’s knowledge, and what is belief versus evidence.
5. A causal/disclosure audit preventing the filing, leak, threat, or legal tactic from becoming the asserted cause of Caleb’s death; historical chronology must remain distinct from audience revelation order.
6. Jurisdiction/date-specific review of the proposed estate clock, will/defective-paper device, custody, settlement, disclosure and publication mechanics.

Leon/Leonard is **not** presently a risk: the reviewed materials consistently use Leon. The break is therefore usable as a provisional design instrument, but it is not continuity-safe to lock, canonize, outline as a pilot, or treat as historical/legal fact.
