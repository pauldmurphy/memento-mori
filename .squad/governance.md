# Canyon House Pictures governance

## Ownership

Paul Murphy is creator and final creative authority. Alex is the accountable creative lead. Lauren provides independent development review. Maya owns continuity records; Charlie owns screenplay sources; Julian and Morgan own their research; Nina owns dramaturgical assessment; Jamie owns deck execution.

## Brief requirements

- Ten episodes; preserve the named team and agent identities.
- Fountain (.fountain), UTF-8, for all screenplay drafts, scene samples, revisions and editable screenplay deliveries. No FDX. PDF reading copies are exported from matching approved Fountain masters.
- Preserve source bytes and separate fiction canon from verified history/legal findings.
- Current phase is development diagnosis pending. Repository setup does not mean the diagnosis or series package is complete.

## Review gates

Paul approves the creative brief before the season break; season architecture before pilot development; pilot outline before screenplay drafting; and the pilot and final package at their milestones. Routine research, notes and revisions proceed within the approved brief.

Lauren reviews substantial story submissions independently. Alex checks package copy or coverage authored by Lauren for creative consistency. A writer cannot provide the only approval of their own work. Specialist research and cultural notes are inputs to review, not substitutes for Paul's judgment or genuine professional consultation.

Distinguish optional suggestions, revision notes and formal blocking findings. A blocking finding names the evidence, affected requirement and minimum resolution. If Squad's reviewer protocol invokes author lockout, follow its reassignment process. Do not label routine creative iteration as a blocking rejection.

## Decisions and records

Accept operational directives already established by Paul; label new creative proposals clearly. Record approval provenance and affected documents. Team agreement informs decisions; Paul remains the final arbiter. Scribe retains its fixed narrow decision-merging contract. Maya records requested meetings and maintains canon ledgers.

## Repository scope

Use a branch and pull request for reviewable changes. Follow .github/PR_REQUIREMENTS.md. Routing, automated checks and agent approval do not authorize merging or publishing. Preserve existing built-in support agents and the installed coordinator's governance outside its SDK-generated capability block.
