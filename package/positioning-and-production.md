## Audience proposition

The entry is immediate: a woman thinks she has caught her father in one final lie. The first pleasure is watching a capable lawyer diagnose her own family while missing the limits of her diagnosis. The will turns siblings, spouse, partner and strangers into people with competing material interests before the historical story is fully known.

The sustained question changes from who the strangers are to what Callie can persuade them to give her. Daniel and Sarah can stop a meeting, keep a letter private or disagree about an account. Their choices make access dramatic. Each answer alters a relationship, a financial position or an available next action, giving the viewer something to anticipate beyond the next revelation.

The historical line supplies its own anticipation: Caleb and Marian disagree about how to protect a future they are actively building. Their humor, work and marital friction must land before danger narrows their options. The two periods produce a specific weekly experience: a present-day maneuver acquires a different meaning after the audience has lived through the older choice.

The audience hypothesis is adults drawn to family power struggles, morally complicated mysteries and historical drama with consequential character choices. This is an editorial proposition, not measured audience demand or a claim of broad appeal. Ten approximately hour-long episodes with a complete ending remains the working presentation format.

| Creative coordinate supplied by Paul | Useful quality | MEMENTO MORI's own expression |
|---|---|---|
| *Succession* | Family affection and hostility expressed through power, money and wit | An estate makes a middle-class/professional family bargain over its dead father's account; wit often conceals a refusal to grieve. No corporate dynasty is required. |
| *Mississippi Burning* | A visceral sense of racial danger and institutional obstruction | The historical household has its own active life and perspective. Caleb, Marian and Leon make choices; their experience is not delivered solely through investigators or a white lawyer's revelation. |

These titles describe tone and approach. They imply no shared rights, producers, attachments, budgets, sales outcome, audience equivalence or buyer interest. The series should be judged on its own family, dual-timeline structure and contest over the record.

## Preliminary production design

| Element | Tangible driver and working approach | Assessment still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Principal ensemble | Callie, Ruth, Leon, the Mori siblings, Daniel and Sarah carry the present; Caleb, Marian and younger Eli/Leon carry the historical line. Selected additional relatives, colleagues and community members must feel socially connected. | Casting and producer review of ensemble size, day-player consolidation and availability. No actor is attached. |
| Age and period casting | The proposed design spans sixty years. Younger and older Eli and Leon need matching behavior as well as appearance. Ruth's historical presence, if used, adds a similar requirement. Present Leon's advanced age should shape blocking and assistance without reducing his authority. | Practical balance of separate casting, makeup, accessible locations and shooting demands. No performer age, prosthetic result or medical capability is assumed. |
| Recurring present interiors | Mori home, kitchen/dining room and office; Leon's work or meeting space; storage unit and Caldwell-controlled private space. | Historical interiors and streets | Caldwell household/work space, small law office and selected community/professional locations; streets must support the chosen 1965 story design. | Period dressing, signage, vehicles, props, background action, visual-effects cleanup and the cost of making any proposed location credible. Story setting does not constitute a location commitment. |
| Archival-looking objects | Original documents, ledgers, cut/uncut photographs, video, pen, award and payment records repeat with changed meanings. The same item must remain physically legible and trackable. | Art department designs, duplicates, readable inserts, actor likeness releases and item-by-item custody continuity. No genuine archival imagery is required by the current concept. |
| Mourning, meals and gatherings | Shiva, a contentious family dinner and community/legal activity create ensemble scenes and controlled interruptions. | Vehicles and violence | Eli's ordinary collision can be communicated through aftermath. Historical threat and Caleb's death require a deliberate camera and sound approach that keeps the interruption of his life central. | Producer, director and relevant department planning for vehicles, stunt work, weapons if any, effects, safety and performer support. Avoid promising either a cheap solution or a spectacle before scene assessment. |
| Music | Original score and any incidental performances can connect domestic intimacy and pressure without using songs as historical proof. | Composition and recording plan; rights and cost assessment for any existing song ultimately selected. No music rights are cleared. |

The two periods are essential to the viewing experience. Their cost cannot be inferred from the amount of dialogue or a small number of recurring rooms. Period exteriors, age work, ensemble scenes, props, violence and schedule fragmentation can change the plan materially. There is no precise budget, episode cost, shoot schedule, financing, incentive assumption or distribution commitment in this package. A human producer and relevant departments must assess the actual script.

## Development and professional limits

The Caldwell case and principal characters are fictional/composite, with approved fictional choices tracked separately from the preserved treatment and researched claims. The historical and present legal mechanisms are dramatic designs, not a finding that an actual filing, remedy or publication entitlement exists. A will contest does not automatically grant access to the Caldwells' records or permission to publish them.

Historical, legal, cultural, archival, music, likeness and publication questions remain professional assessment matters. No clearance, consultation, endorsement, attachment, sale or outreach has occurred. Research and consultation are advisory under Paul's creative standard; honest disclosure of their status remains mandatory. The consolidated [issue register](unresolved-issues.md), when finalized, is the delivery source for remaining professional and rights matters.
